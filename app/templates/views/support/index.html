Support

You can get online support for the Emergency Alerts Service 24 hours a day.

Reporting an emergency

An issue is an emergency if:

  • you are the only person on call and cannot sign in
  • no one on your team who is on call can sign in
  • you see a 'technical difficulties' error message
  • you see a '500 response code' error message

If you have an emergency, do not use the support form. You must follow your 'standard operating procedures' to raise a PagerDuty alert with the on-call support team.

For all other issues

If your problem is not an emergency, complete the support form.

We aim to reply by the end of the next working day.
